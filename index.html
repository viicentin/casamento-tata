Travel & Stay

Everything you need to know for the trip

Getting There

Fly to Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS). Direct flights available from most major cities. We recommend arriving 1-2 days early.

Where to Stay

The Uluwatu area has wonderful resorts and villas. We'll share a curated list of recommended accommodations closer to the date.

Weather

May is the start of dry season in Bali. Expect warm days around 28-30°C with beautiful sunsets over the ocean.

FAQ

Do I need a visa to travel to Bali?

Most nationalities can obtain a Visa on Arrival (VOA) at the airport for stays up to 30 days. Please check the latest requirements for your country.

Can I bring my children?

While we love your little ones, we have chosen to make this an adults-only celebration so everyone can relax and enjoy the evening.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony will begin in the late afternoon to catch the sunset. We will share the detailed schedule closer to the date.

Is there transportation from hotels?

We will arrange shuttle service from recommended hotels to the venue. Details will be shared once accommodations are confirmed.
